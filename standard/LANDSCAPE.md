Compiled 2026-11-04 for ABS v0.3. Statuses updated from Independent Audit 2 (`audits/AUDIT_2_REGULATORY.md`, primary sources read 2026-11-04); its source keys S1 to S49 are listed in section 6. Every row names its source and the date it was read.

How to read the status column:
- **verified (Audit 0)**: checked on the primary text by the independent auditor on 2026-11-04
  (`audits/AUDIT_0_THESIS.md`), or by its research lanes with the source given there.
- **verified (Audit 2)**: checked on the primary text by the regulatory audit of 2026-11-04 (source key in section 6).
- **verified (lane)**: checked by this document's web research lanes on 2026-11-04; the lane's note
  is summarised in the row.
- **pending**: the research lane was asked to confirm the scope and had not reported when this
  version was written; the URL is the standard's official location and the scope statement is kept
  to what the standard's title states.
- **UNVERIFIED**: could not be confirmed from a primary or reputable source; kept only because the
  parties ABS must work with name it.

Nothing in the ABS text depends on a pending or UNVERIFIED row beyond what the row itself says.

**Where the gaps are.** No protocol above defines an agent-specific dispute reason code, a
decision order for "authorised but wrong", an evidence level standard, or delivery evidence; AP2
says dispute use is out of scope; Visa allocates in-mandate agent actions to the cardholder and
Amex to the issuer; the A2A schemes are building scam protection, not conformity protection. ABS
is built in those gaps (ABS v0.2, A8.1).
