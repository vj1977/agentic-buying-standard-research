# Executive summary

- **Workshop recommendation: reject the current paper, invite resubmission after methodological and implementation corrections.** The useful contribution is a dispute benchmark showing that convincing evidence fabrication defeats otherwise accurate judges. The broader claims about reliable adjudication are not established.
- **The principal Phase 3 counts reproduce:** Fable 12/14, Grok 14/14, hybrid 6/14. Across 146 stored attempts, I found no discrepancy in saved success flags or changes outside the permitted evidence fields.
- **The attacker explicitly receives ground truth and success feedback.** This is disclosed, not covert leakage. I found no direct ground-truth field in reconstructed arbiter packages. Describe the experiment as an oracle-informed attack evaluation.
- **“The hybrid halved attack success” is not a controlled architecture comparison.** A1 received ten attempts per case; A2 received five. At five attempts, the comparison is 10/14 versus 6/14, with descriptive paired McNemar **p = 0.125**.
- **Phase 3b does not establish the advertised provenance defence.** Its headline comparison uses historically selected successful attacks against fresh calls, and most rejections are output-format failures. Gate-aware adaptive attacks were not completed.
- **The provenance gate has reproducible bypasses.** A nonexistent registry citation passes. Rewording a non-conformity claim can bypass the denial safeguard. Signed or chained records are also incorrectly conflated with independent evidence.
- **The hybrid does not implement the paper’s confidence threshold.** Two recorded erroneous merchant refunds depend on quantity findings at confidence 0.60 and 0.55.
- **Audit corrections have not propagated throughout the package.** The current standard, executable rulebook, fact-finder prompt, capture-duty branch, settlement instructions and withdrawal logic still disagree.
- **The simulation and capture probe overstate their coverage.** The simulator uses stipulated world facts, counts escalation as decidability, and accepts substring matches as conformity. The “40/40” capture checker accepts a materially defective mandate.
